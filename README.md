
# My Favorite Movie: Apocalypto

## Introduction
**Apocalypto** is an adventure and action movie released in 2006. It was directed by *Mel Gibson* and tells the story of a young Mayan hunter named Jaguar Paw.

## Movie Information
- **Title:** Apocalypto
- **Release Year:** 2006
- **Director:** Mel Gibson
- **Genre:** Action, Adventure, Drama
- **Main Character:** Jaguar Paw

## Movie Story
The movie follows Jaguar Paw, who tries to escape after his village is attacked by a group of warriors. He must survive and return to his family.

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because of its *intense action scenes*, survival story, and realistic portrayal of ancient Mayan civilization.

### My Favorite Things About the Movie
1. Exciting chase scenes
2. Unique ancient setting
3. Strong survival storyline
4. Unexpected events

## My Rating
**10/10 - Highly Recommended!**

> Survival is not just about strength, but also courage and determination.
